Questions people ask

Do I need a CPA or is tax software enough?

Software works for simple returns. Once you own a business, rent property, get an IRS letter or fall behind, a CPA usually saves more than they cost and keeps you out of trouble.

I haven't filed in years. Is it too late?

No. File the missing returns, starting with the most recent ones the IRS expects. Filing stops the biggest penalty from growing even if you can't pay right away.

Can I get help if I can't pay what I owe?

Yes. The IRS and the Colorado Department of Revenue both offer payment plans, and penalties can sometimes be reduced. Getting current on filing comes first.

Do you help outside Denver?

Yes, statewide: Colorado Springs, Fort Collins, Boulder, Grand Junction, Pueblo, the mountain towns and everywhere in between. Most of the work happens securely online.
