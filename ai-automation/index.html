Where accounting is heading

Industry coverage in 2026 describes agentic AI reaching a tipping point in tax and accounting firms, with AI handling more of the intake, prep and admin work, and staff time shifting toward advisory work. Firms that get their front office automated first are the ones prospects reach first.

Questions people ask

Does the AI give tax advice?

No. It answers general questions from an approved knowledge base, qualifies the lead and books time with a licensed professional. Advice stays with the CPA.

Is client data safe?

A live setup keeps intake to the minimum needed to book, sends sensitive documents through the firm's secure portal, and follows the firm's data and confidentiality policies.

Can it connect to our existing tools?

Yes. Typical connections include the firm's calendar, CRM or practice management system, email and client portal, using triggers and actions.
